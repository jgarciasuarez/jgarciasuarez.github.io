# Portfolio Waves

This document is a visual collage that illustrates different wave propagation phenomena and their applications, categorized into four main sections:

1. **General Structure**: 
   - Displays mathematical formulations for wave propagation involving transfer matrices.
   - Shows a differential equation system and its exponential solution relating to $SL_2(\mathbb{R})$ and $sl_2(\mathbb{R})$ groups.
   - References "Garcia-Suarez (2022) JMPS".

2. **Acoustics**: 
   - Shows a submarine equipped with an "anti-sonar coating (anechoic tile)".
   - Presents the equation for absorption efficiency: $A = 1 - |R| - |T| \approx 1$.

3. **Optics**: 
   - Illustrates a "color filter" composed of layered media interacting with "Incoming light".
   - Includes a graph showing transmission peaking at a wavelength of 530 nm.

4. **Elasticity**: 
   - Depicts an "Engineered soil barrier" designed to protect a building from seismic waves.
   - Highlights the concept of "Low-frequency bandgaps" where the layered barrier attenuates incoming wave energy.
