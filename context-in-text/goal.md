# Goal

To create a professional-looking webpage, that screams both 
- elite "associate prof" level researcher
- "we should hire this guy to train our young associates" in a big tech company, a unicorn startup, a commodity trading company or an investment bank.

There should be three main tabs: 
- Bio: brief summary of education, experience and contribution to date, with links to both GitHub and X profiles. Add a wink: I am a native of Seville, Spain, the most beautiful city in the world.
- Research Portfolio: a distillation from my research statement in an appealing visual style. Include hyperlinks to relevant resources.
- Teaching Porfolio: a distillation from my research statement (just text, not images). Include hyperlinks to relevant resources.
- DDCF: this must be the entry point to where to find all the information about my current prohject. This page must be visually complex: there must be a center item with the title
("DDCF" and the full title "Data-Driven Computational Friction") and the SNSF logo, by clicking on it we should open a pop-up window that include the details from the project
and links to the official webpage by SNSF (https://data.snf.ch/grants/grant/216341). Likewise, from this central nods there should be connections going to peripheral nodes (data-driven constitutive modeling,
learning neural operators, GPU-acceleration, Automatic Differentiation), clicking on those should open windows that describe aspects of each subproject. I will provide more detail later on.

The overall theme of the web must slick and dark, the classic color scheme for coding interfaces (dark mode).
