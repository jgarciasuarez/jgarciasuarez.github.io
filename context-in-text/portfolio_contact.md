# Portfolio Contact

This document is a visual collage that illustrates different contact mechanics phenomena across various length scales, represented by an axis ranging from $10^{-9}$ to $10^3$.

The collage features three main panels:
1. **Nanoscale Wear ($10^{-9}$ - $10^{-6}$)**: 
   - Displays a schematic of "shear" and "contact" with annotations for "plasticity?" and "fracture?". 
   - Shows graphs plotting measured volume against predicted volume and work ($W_f$). 
   - References "G.S. et al., JMPS (2022)".

2. **Haptics ($10^{-3}$ - $10^0$)**: 
   - Displays a finger touching a surface.
   - Shows a graph of contact radius ($r_{contact}$) versus velocity ($V$).
   - Highlights a schematic showing a soft interface, emphasizing elasticity, inertia, viscosity, and air bubble entrapment causing contact area loss over time.
   - References "Bilotto et al., JFM (2025)".

3. **Data-driven Friction ($10^3$)**: 
   - Shows a geological scale representation (1km) depicting tectonic or large-scale friction.
   - Shows a graph of normalized velocity protocols (targets vs. predictions) for rate-and-state friction data.
   - References "Cortes and G.S., AC&G (2025)".
