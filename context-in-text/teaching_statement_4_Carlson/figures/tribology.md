This figure presents data and models related to tribology and wear volume. 
- Panel (a) illustrates three cross-sectional schematics of asperity contacts between two rough surfaces during sliding, highlighting regions of overlap and potential wear particle formation.
- Panel (b) is a scatter plot comparing "predicted $V_d$" versus "measured $V_d$" (wear volume), showing data points that align well with a dashed 1:1 line. An inset shows a 3D simulation of a breaking asperity.
- Panel (c) is a log-log plot of "measured $V_d$" against a parameter "$W_t$". It shows two distinct scaling regimes with dashed lines indicating proportionalities to $W_t$ and $W_t^{3/2}$. An inset image shows a corresponding wear particle from a simulation.
