# collector.png Description

This schematic diagram represents an articulated structure consisting of five grey rectangular segments (or bodies) connected in series by hinges.

- The hinges are indicated by vertical black lines connected to ground symbols (fixed supports) at the bottom.
- At each hinge point between the segments, there is a rotational degree of freedom.
- These degrees of freedom are denoted by angles `\theta_1, \theta_2, \theta_3, \theta_4, \theta_5`.
- Curved blue arrows at each hinge indicate the direction of rotation.
- The rightmost segment is additionally attached to a fixed support on its right side.
