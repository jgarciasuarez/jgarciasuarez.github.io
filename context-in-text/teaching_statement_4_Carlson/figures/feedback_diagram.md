# feedback_diagram.pdf Description

This block diagram illustrates the feedback and information flow between three main project work packages or structural themes, represented by horizontal colored bars divided into numbered subsections.

- **Top Row (Blue):** Contains five subsections labeled 1.1, 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, and 1.5.
- **Middle Row (Red):** Contains three subsections labeled 2.1, 2.2, and 2.3.
- **Bottom Row (Orange):** Contains two subsections, both labeled 3.1 (which may be a typo for 3.1 and 3.2).

**Information Flow (Arrows):**
- **Data:** Passed downwards from 1.2 to 2.1, and downwards from 2.1 to the first 3.1. It is also passed upwards from 2.2 to 1.4, and from 2.3 to 1.5.
- **Code:** Passed downwards from the second 3.1 (or 3.2) to 2.1, and from 1.5 to 2.3.
- **Guideline:** Several upwards-pointing arrows indicate guidelines being passed up the hierarchy. For example, from 3.1 up to 2.2 and 2.3, and from 2.2/2.3 up to 1.3, 1.4, and 1.5.
