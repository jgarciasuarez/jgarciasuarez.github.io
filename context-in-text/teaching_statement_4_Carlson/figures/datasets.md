# datasets.pdf Description

This image contains two main panels side by side, illustrating the behavior of granular materials under pressure.

**Left Panel:**
- Shows a vertical column composed of a simulated granular material, extending from a top line labeled "surface" to a bottom line labeled "bedrock".
- To the right of the column is a shaded wedge pointing downwards. It transitions from blue at the top to red at the bottom and is labeled "pressure", visually indicating that pressure increases with depth towards the bedrock.

**Right Panel:**
- Displays a scatter plot of shear stress `\tau [Pa]` on the y-axis versus shear strain `\gamma [-]` on the x-axis.
- Several data series are plotted using dots in different colors ranging from blue to red.
- An upward-pointing arrow labeled "increasing pressure" shows that as pressure increases (from blue data points to red data points), the slope of the stress-strain curve (representing the shear modulus) also increases.
- An inset diagram within the plot illustrates a sheared rectangular block of granular particles, with horizontal arrows at the top and bottom indicating the direction of applied shear.
