## Profile
### Brief research statement
I am a mechanician with industry experience who uses theory, high–performance computing, and data–driven methods to tackle problems in interface mechanics, wave propagation, and their numerical modeling. My broad training in physics and applied mathematics enables original work focused on developing predictive tools to be deployed in practical settings. In the words of Prof. Hans Liepmann: “understanding of fundamentals with an appreciation for applications.”

### Main interests
- **Contact problems**: Fluid-solid interation (FSI), lubrication theory, friction modeling, wear prediction
- **Computational mechanics**: Data-driven methods, GPU adaptation
- **Acoustic, elastic and optical metamaterials**: Wave propagation in layered media, algebraic approaches
