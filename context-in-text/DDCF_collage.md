# DDCF Collage

The image is a collage illustrating various research themes under the project "DDCF" (Data-driven computational friction) funded by the Swiss National Science Foundation.

The collage is divided into several thematic sections:
1. **Data-driven friction**: (Top left) Shows a graph plotting targets vs. predictions for friction along with a spring-block model diagram, illustrating kinetic energy, strain energy, and frictional dissipation.
2. **Neural-Operator Dynamic Friction Constitutive Modeling**: (Center) Large text highlighting the DDCF acronym and the Swiss National Science Foundation logo.
3. **Haptics and Contact**: (Top right) Asks "Tune contact through shape?" with diagrams of a finger interacting with a surface. It details mechanisms like elasticity, inertia, viscosity, and air bubble entrapment causing contact area loss over time ($\sim \mu s$).
4. **Differentiable-physics Optimization for Meta-interfaces**: (Bottom left) Shows a 3D rendering of a bumpy, pillared surface meta-interface.
5. **Wave Propagation Metamaterials**: (Bottom center/right) Shows a submarine with "anti-sonar coating (anechoic tile)".
6. **GPU solvers for Elastohydrodynamic Lubrication**: (Bottom right) Text highlighting the use of GPU solvers.
