# Panel: Data-Driven Friction (v2)

This figure is a variation focusing on macroscopic data-driven friction ($10^0$ to $10^3$ length scales), with an emphasis on energy conversion during sliding.

Key elements include:
- A schematic of a **spring-block model** representing stick-slip dynamics. It illustrates a block with "kinetic energy" driven by a spring storing "strain energy," which is converted into "frictional dissipation."
- A graph showing "rate-and-state friction data," plotting friction coefficient ($\mu$) against normalized time ($t/T$).
- The plot shows the dynamic response to an "evolving sliding velocity protocol," comparing training targets (black markers) against a model prediction (red line) and a noiseless target (grey line). 
- Reference: Cortes and G.S., AC&G (2025).
