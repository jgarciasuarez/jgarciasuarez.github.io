# Panel: Data-Driven Friction

This figure focuses on macroscopic friction phenomena spanning length scales from $10^0$ to $10^3$ meters. 

The panel features:
- A geological block diagram illustrating tectonic plates sliding past one another in opposite directions (credit: Tasa Graphics Art, 1998).
- A detailed graph showing "rate-and-state friction data" plotted as the coefficient of friction ($\mu$) against normalized time ($t/T$). 
- The plot compares "targets (training)" (black dots), "prediction" (red line), and "noiseless target" (grey line).
- The friction coefficient responds to an "evolving sliding velocity protocol" overlaid on the graph, capturing complex stick-slip dynamics and velocity-dependent friction behavior.
