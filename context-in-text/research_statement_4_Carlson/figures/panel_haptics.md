# Panel: Haptics

This figure highlights phenomena at the haptics scale, spanning length scales roughly from $10^{-6}$ to $10^{-3}$ meters.

The panel features:
- A diagram of a human finger interacting with a surface, setting the context of human touch.
- A graph plotting contact radius ($r_{contact}$ [mm]) against velocity ($V$ [m/s]). It compares experimental data and simulation results for both "soft" and "hard" materials, divided into regimes based on a parameter $\phi$ ($\phi \ll 1$, $\phi \sim 1$, $\phi \gg 1$).
- A multi-stage schematic illustrating the physics of impact over microsecond timescales (time $\sim \mu$s). It shows a curved surface approaching a flat plane through a fluid.
- The diagram highlights the interplay of "elasticity," "inertia," and fluid "viscosity," culminating in an "air bubble entrapment" that results in "contact area loss" (reference: Bilotto et al., JFM 2025).
