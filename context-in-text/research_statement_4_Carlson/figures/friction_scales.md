# Friction Scales

This figure presents a timeline-style progression of friction and wear phenomena across a wide range of length scales, represented by an axis from $10^{-9}$ to $10^3$. 

From left to right, it highlights four distinct regimes:
1. **$10^{-9}$ scale:** Labeled "nanoscale debris creation, adhesive wear," illustrated with a molecular dynamics simulation showing atoms and nanoparticle debris between two sliding surfaces.
2. **$10^{-6}$ scale:** Labeled "roughness evolution," accompanied by a 3D topographic map of a rough surface with peaks and valleys.
3. **$10^{-3}$ to $10^0$ scale:** Labeled "fluid-meditated solid impact," showing a schematic of a solid sphere moving downwards towards a surface through a fluid medium.
4. **$10^0$ to $10^3$ scale:** Labeled "rate-and-state friction, stick-slip dynamics," illustrated with a macro-scale geological block diagram showing tectonic plates sliding past each other (image credited to Tasa Graphics Art, 1998).
